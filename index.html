Our Guiding Principles

Why We Built My Kind Karma.

Generosity is innate to the human spirit. In 2024, Americans gave over $592 billion to charity. Yet for so many, giving feels fragmented, cumbersome, or obscured by administrative overhead. We're here to change that.

🕊️

Simplicity First

Giving shouldn't require creating ten different accounts across ten different portals. Discover, give, and organize everything in one clean home.

🛡️

Uncompromising Trust

Every organization on our platform is continuously verified against the IRS 501(c)(3) tax-exempt database. You can always give with full peace of mind.

🌱

Zero Platform Skimming

We don't take a cut of your gift. Standard credit card processing goes straight to the processor, and platform operations are sustained by voluntary patron gifts.
