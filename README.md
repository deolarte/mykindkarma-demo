# My Kind Karma™ — Redesigned Platform & Corporate Showcase

A purpose-driven, high-trust redesign for **My Kind Karma** (NC 501(c)(3) Nonprofit, EIN: 42-3740341) positioning the organization for workplace giving, corporate gift matching, and conscious donors.

## Features Included

- **Warm, Purpose-Driven Theme**: Editorial typography (`Lora` serif), soft warm alabaster and pebble stone backgrounds (`#FAF8F5`), and natural forest sage accents (`#2E5644`).
- **Interactive Impact Simulator**: Live sliders for company size, matching allowance, and participation rate with real-time community meal and tax deduction computations.
- **Re-engineered `#what` Matrix**: Three distinct audience lenses:
  - 🏢 Companies & Purpose-Driven Teams
  - 🌱 Individuals & Employee Donors
  - 🤝 501(c)(3) Nonprofit Partners
- **Fiduciary Governance**: Board and fiduciary showcase (Arti Sheth, Kevin Paro, Fahd Vahidy).
- **Corporate Inquiry Flow**: Dedicated B2B partner contact forms.

## Deploying to GitHub Pages

1. Go to your repository on GitHub.
2. Click **Settings** > **Pages** (in the left sidebar).
3. Under **Build and deployment**:
   - **Source**: `Deploy from a branch`
   - **Branch**: Select `main` and folder `/ (root)`
4. Click **Save**.
5. Your live public demo will be accessible at: `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`
